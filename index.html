How a project runs

Process
  1. 01 · BRIEFOne call, one page

    Your goal, your audience, the platforms and three videos you like.

  2. 02 · SCRIPTWritten in the right language

    Arabic is written in Arabic from the start, never translated line by line.

  3. 03 · LOOKStyle frames first

    You approve the look on still frames before a single second is animated.

  4. 04 · BUILDVoice, music, motion

    Narration, an original score and the edit, with two rounds of changes.

  5. 05 · DELIVEREvery platform

    Masters for 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1, captions, and a cover for each.

The studio

About

Fanus (فانوس) is Arabic for lantern: a small light that makes people stop and look. That's the whole job.

Fanus Studio is run by Abdullah Habta, a video producer working in Arabic and English since 2015. Everything on this page comes from the studio's own channels: Red Pencil, AlArabi, Arab ARTPOP, Pip & the Hum and Hollow Hill Nights.

We work remotely with brands, agencies and creators in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and the wider Gulf, and with YouTube channels everywhere.

We use AI tools for parts of the visuals, music and voice. A person writes, directs and edits every frame, and every fact in a documentary is checked against a source.
